Hello there! 👋 

I am a Ph.D. student at the [HoWNet Team](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/LIP/index.php/research/hownet-team) within the [LIP Laboratory](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/LIP/) at [École Normale Supérieure (ENS) Lyon](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/), advised by [Prof. Francesco Bronzino](https://fbronzino.com/) and [Prof. Anthony Busson](https://anthonybusson.fr/).

My research focuses on the intersection of machine learning, network traffic analysis, and dynamic traffic processing systems.
I design adaptive systems that optimize performance under real-world conditions, addressing challenges such as real-time packet filtering, efficient traffic analysis, and system self-monitoring.
These systems aim to assist network administrators and researchers by improving network monitoring and enhancing operational efficiency.
